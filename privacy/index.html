Privacy Policy

This website does not collect personal information. The email signup form on the home page and the contact form are placeholders — they do not send or store anything yet, and this policy will be updated when they are connected.

Pages on this site load no third-party trackers. The currency converter on the Money page fetches exchange rates from a public rate service (exchangerate-api.com) when you use it; no personal data is sent with that request.

External links (for example to Google Maps, Agoda or Booking.com) open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.
