About

Trip Handbook Vietnam is a free travel guide for international visitors to Vietnam. It covers 13 destinations — Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Ninh Binh, Sa Pa, Phong Nha, Hue, Da Nang, Hoi An, Nha Trang, Da Lat, Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta and Phu Quoc — with things to do, food, stays, transport, budgets and safety for each one.

Every place in the guides has a verified address and a Google Maps link. Where a price, address or opening hour could not be verified, the guide says so instead of guessing. Prices were checked in November 2026.

A free companion app with full offline navigation is planned for later; the website is the first step.
