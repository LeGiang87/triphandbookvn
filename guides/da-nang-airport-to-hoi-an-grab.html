How to Get from Da Nang Airport to Hoi An by Grab (and What It Costs in 2026)

Hoi An has no airport. Every flight lands at Da Nang International Airport (DAD), about 30 km north of the Old Town, and the last leg is a 30 to 45 minute drive south. Grab is the way most first-timers make that drive: the price is fixed in the app before you confirm, there is no haggling at the curb, and the trip is recorded. Book it right and you are at your hotel for about 250,000 to 350,000 VND. Book it wrong and you are the person at arrivals paying 800,000 VND to a man who is not your driver.

The short answer: install Grab before you fly, and have mobile data working when you land — a local SIM or an eSIM. After baggage claim, walk past the taxi queue to the marked ride-hailing pickup zone, set your hotel as the destination, and check the price before you confirm. A 4-seat GrabCar usually costs 250,000 to 350,000 VND (about $10–14) in normal traffic, and a 7-seat car 350,000 to 450,000 VND. Keep 15,000 VND in small cash for the airport entrance fee, which is added on top of the app fare. Match the license plate before you put your bags in the trunk.

The province guides on this site quote 300,000–400,000 VND for a Grab or taxi on this route, which sits inside the same band once surge pricing is counted.

How to book the Grab, step by step

1. Install Grab and sort out data before you fly

Grab is the same idea as Uber: one app, cars and motorbikes, the price shown before you confirm. Download it at home and set up payment there, because the airport is the worst place to fight with an app store. You need mobile data to book — airport Wi-Fi is patchy at the curb. A local SIM at the airport or an eSIM bought before you fly both work; without data, Grab is not an option at all, and you will be choosing between the taxi queue and a pre-booked car instead. Install Xanh SM too, as a backup — the blue electric taxis run the same route for a similar price and sometimes have cars when Grab is surging.

2. Set your hotel as the destination, not "Hoi An"

Type the hotel name and pick the exact address from the list, or paste the address from your booking confirmation. "Hoi An" on its own drops the pin in the middle of the Old Town, where cars are banned from the main streets during pedestrian hours — your driver will call you to ask where you actually are, and you will be standing in the wrong place. If your hotel is inside the Old Town, expect the driver to stop at the edge and walk the last few minutes with you; that is normal, not a problem with the booking.

3. Pick the right car size before you confirm

A standard GrabCar seats four people with hand luggage. Two large suitcases plus three adults do not fit, and a driver who arrives to find they cannot take your bags may cancel. Book GrabCar 7 for five or six people, or for four people with big suitcases. There is no child seat option — if you are travelling with a small child, bring your own seat or book a private car and ask for one in advance.

4. Read the price, then confirm

The app shows the full fare before you tap confirm. In normal traffic that is about 250,000–350,000 VND for a 4-seat car. In heavy rain or at peak hours it can climb to 450,000–500,000 VND. If the number looks high, wait five minutes and check again, or compare with Xanh SM — the blue electric taxis — which run the same route for a similar price and sometimes have cars when Grab is surging. What you see is what you pay for the ride itself, with one exception below.

5. Keep 15,000 VND in small cash for the airport fee

Every car entering or leaving Da Nang Airport pays a small entrance fee, usually 10,000 to 15,000 VND. It is not in the app quote. The driver will ask you to pay it in cash at the exit barrier, or add it to the final fare. This is a real fee, not a scam — do not argue about 15,000 VND at the barrier.

6. Pay cash or by card

You can pay in cash (Vietnamese dong) or by card in the app. Some guides note Grab adds about a 4% fee on foreign cards, so cash is usually a few thousand dong cheaper. Either way, agree nothing extra with the driver: the app fare plus the airport fee is the whole price.

Where to find your driver at Da Nang Airport

This is the part most guides skip, and it is where first-timers lose money. Grab drivers cannot pick you up at the arrivals doors. There is a marked ride-hailing pickup zone a short walk from the hall — about 50 metres, following the signs — and that is where your driver will be.

  • International terminal (T2): cross the first two lanes of traffic to the designated pickup area near the parking lot entrance.
  • Domestic terminal (T1): the pickup zone is to the left as you exit, near the concrete pillars in the middle island. Drivers often message a pillar number through the in-app chat — "Pillar 4" or "Column 5" — so watch your messages and go to that pillar.
  • Match the plate before you get in. Check the license plate in the app, then check the car. Touts stand near the pickup point and claim to be your driver — some even show you a Grab app on their own phone. They are not your driver. If the plate does not match, do not put your bags in the trunk.

The one mistake to avoid: the touts at arrivals

Outside the arrivals hall, men who are not licensed drivers will approach you asking "Taxi? Hoi An?" and quote 600,000 to 1,200,000 VND — double or triple the real price. Some say the Grab app is broken, or that they are Grab and charge the same price. They are not. Ignore them, walk past the taxi queue to the ride-hailing zone, and book in your own app. The legitimate alternatives are right there too: the official taxi rank is the row of green Mai Linh or Vinasun cabs at the arrivals curb, meter on, about 300,000–550,000 VND to Hoi An. Use those fleets, not an unmarked car.

A second, smaller trap: a driver who messages you through the app asking for an extra "return fee" because he has to drive back to Da Nang empty. The app fare is the fare. If a driver insists on extra money beyond the airport fee, cancel and rebook — there will be another car in minutes.

When Grab is the wrong tool for this trip

Grab is right for most people, most of the time. It fails in four situations:

  • No data. No SIM, no eSIM, no booking. Sort out data before you land, or pre-book a private car.
  • After midnight. Supply thins out noticeably after midnight. If your flight lands very late, a pre-booked car that waits with a name board is worth the same money.
  • Big group or big luggage. More than four people, or suitcases that will not fit a sedan — book a 7-seat car or a private transfer.
  • Heavy rain at rush hour. Surge pricing can push a Grab past the price of a fixed private car. Check both before you confirm.

Getting back: Hoi An to Da Nang Airport

The return trip costs the same band — about 250,000–350,000 VND for a 4-seat GrabCar — and you book it the same way. Two differences. First, there are fewer Grab drivers in Hoi An than in Da Nang, so at peak times expect a 10 to 15 minute wait; book 15 minutes earlier than you think you need to. Second, leave a real buffer: the drive is 30 to 45 minutes, but allow a full hour plus check-in time, more if it is raining. Your hotel can also arrange a car for about the same price — ask when you check in, and confirm the price is for the car, not per person.

Grab or something else? Choose in 3 questions

Answer these three and the choice picks itself:

1. Do you have mobile data when you land?

Yes, and you want the cheapest door-to-door ride — take Grab. No, and you do not want to buy a SIM at the airport — take a metered taxi from the official rank, or pre-book a private car before you fly.

2. What time do you land?

Daytime or evening — Grab, in minutes. After midnight, or with small children and a lot of bags — a pre-booked car waiting with your name on a board is worth the same money for far less stress.

3. How many of you are there?

One or two, travelling light — Grab, or the shared shuttle if every dong counts. Five or six, or four with big suitcases — a 7-seat Grab or a private car, so nobody is left standing at the curb while the driver cancels.

The bottom line

Da Nang Airport to Hoi An is a 30 km, 30 to 45 minute drive, and Grab is the simplest way to do it: 250,000–350,000 VND for a standard car in normal traffic, fixed in the app before you confirm. Install the app before you fly, have data when you land, walk to the marked pickup zone, match the plate, and keep 15,000 VND cash for the airport fee. The only wrong answer is the one that starts with a stranger at the arrivals curb asking if you need a taxi.

Places on this route, with addresses

Da Nang International Airport (DAD)

  • Address: Hoa Thuan Tay Ward, Hai Chau District, Da Nang
  • Google Maps: Open in Google Maps
  • Price: Grab to the city centre 50,000–90,000 VND (~$2–3.50); metered taxi 60,000–150,000 VND (~$2.30–5.80), plus a small airport exit fee of 10,000–15,000 VND (~$0.40–0.60)
  • Hours: Open 24 hours
  • Type: station
  • Tip: Ride-hailing pick-up is in a designated zone about 50 m from the arrivals hall — follow the signs instead of taking the first taxi that approaches you inside the terminal.

Hoi An Ancient Town

  • Address: Tran Phu Street, Hoi An Ancient Town, Da Nang
  • Google Maps: Open in Google Maps
  • Price: Walking the streets is free. Since 10 August 2026, entry to each heritage building costs 20,000 VND (~$0.80) per person, the same for Vietnamese and foreign visitors; children under 16 free. The old 120,000 VND ticket is no longer sold.
  • Hours: Ticket booths 07:00–21:30 in summer, to 21:00 in winter; heritage sites about 08:30–17:30
  • Type: attraction
  • Tip: The 20,000 VND ticket covers one of 22 heritage sites — the assembly halls, old houses and museums. Buy tickets only at the official booths. On the full-moon lantern night each month, the electric lights go off around 18:00–22:00 and the town glows by lantern alone; remaining 2026 dates are 22 November and 22 December.
