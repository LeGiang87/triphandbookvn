Smart Maps & Offline Navigation

Web vs app: full offline navigation lives in the app later. On this website you can read downloaded content and use simple maps — download the offline maps below before you travel.

Offline maps — how to download

Google Maps (offline areas)

  1. Open Google Maps and search for the city or area (for example, “Tam Coc, Ninh Binh”).
  2. Tap the place name at the bottom, then Download offline map (or your profile picture → Offline maps → Select your own map).
  3. Zoom to cover the whole area you will visit, then download over Wi-Fi.
  4. Download each destination's map before you leave your hotel — the guides name Google Maps as the navigation app to use in every province.

Maps.me (fully offline)

  1. Install Maps.me and download the Vietnam map (and the map of each region you will visit) over Wi-Fi before you travel.
  2. It works with GPS alone, no mobile data needed — useful on boats, in mountains and in the countryside where signal is weak.

Street Food Safety Guide

How to choose a safe street-food spot — the rule used in the guides: pick places where you can see the kitchen, ingredients are covered, local customers are eating, and prices are listed on a menu. This site only marks a restaurant as meeting hygiene standards when it passes specific, checkable criteria like these — never on a feeling.

Hidden Gems

Quieter, local places from the guides — not the main tourist circuit.

Ha Long

Hidden gems and quieter water

Ninh Binh

Hidden gems

Sa Pa

Hidden gem — Ta Phin village. About 12 km east of town, home to Red Dao families famous for herbal baths. A soak in a wooden tub of medicinal herbs costs roughly 150,000–200,000 VND (~$6–8) — perfect after a long trek. Go by motorbike or arrange a car through your hotel; the village is much quieter than Cat Cat.

Da Lat

Hidden gems. The flower gardens at the Valley of Love and the hydrangea fields on the city's edge are at their best in the dry season. For something different, visit a coffee farm on the road to Langbiang — weasel coffee and arabica tastings run about 100,000–200,000 VND (~$4–8) per person, and the farm setting beats any city cafe. Ask your hotel for a farm that welcomes visitors rather than turning up unannounced.

Can Tho & Mekong Delta

Hidden gem — the small canals after the market. Most visitors go straight back to the hotel after Cai Rang. Instead, ask your boat driver to take you through the small canals behind the market for another hour: stilt houses, children going to school by boat, women washing vegetables at the water's edge. That quiet hour is the Delta at its most real.

Photo Spots — best light

Check-in spots from the guides, with the best time of day for light.

Hanoi

Best light and photo times

  • Hoan Kiem Lake: 06:00 for tai chi and mist, and again at dusk when the Turtle Tower lights up.
  • The Old Quarter: early morning, before the motorbikes take over, and at night when the lanterns and signs come on.
  • Temple of Literature: 08:00-09:00, before the tour groups arrive.
  • West Lake and Tran Quoc Pagoda: late afternoon to sunset.
  • Train Street: golden hour, with the cafes lit and a train due after 19:00 on weekdays.

Ha Long

Best light and photo times

  • On the bay: early morning, before the day boats arrive — the reason to sleep on an overnight cruise. Late afternoon golden hour is the second-best light.
  • Ti Top Island summit: late afternoon, for the view over the bay in warm light.
  • The Queen Cable Car: sunset, looking back over Cua Luc Bay and the city.
  • Bai Chay Beach: sunset, with the karsts on the horizon.
  • Quang Ninh Museum: daytime, for the black glass facade reflecting the sea and sky.

Ninh Binh

Map clusters and best light

  • Tam Coc cluster (stay, eat, cycle): Tam Coc Boat Pier, Bich Dong Pagoda, Thai Vi Temple, Hang Mua, Thung Nham. Street food and local restaurants; cycle early in the morning; stay in a homestay with a field view.
  • Trang An - Hoa Lu cluster (heritage): Trang An Boat Pier (pier coordinates about 20.2537, 105.9187), Hoa Lu Ancient Capital, Am Tien Cave. Hidden gem: Am Tien in the morning; Hoa Lu history; Trang An boat early in the morning.
  • Northern cluster (spiritual + nature): Bai Dinh Pagoda, Van Long Lagoon. Bai Dinh before 08:30; Van Long at sunset.
  • Western cluster (forest): Cuc Phuong National Park. A full day in the forest; butterfly season around April-May.

Best photo times

  • Hang Mua summit (whole Tam Coc view): sunrise and 16:30 to sunset. In rice season, late May to early June, it is the best frame of the year.
  • On the Tam Coc boat: 07:30-09:00. Low angled sun, the rice fields pick up color, few boats coming the other way into your frame.
  • Trang An: 07:00-08:30. Early mist on the water between the cliffs.
  • Lotus lake at the foot of Hang Mua: early morning in June-July. Lotus blooms best in the morning and closes as the midday sun grows.
  • Van Long Lagoon: 15:30 to sunset. The water is flat as a mirror, birds flying home to roost.
  • Bich Dong Pagoda: before 08:00. Few visitors, incense smoke and early sun through the gate.

Sa Pa

Photo spots and best light: Muong Hoa Valley at sunrise (mist in the valley, 6:00–7:30); the Fansipan summit on a clear morning before 10:00; Cat Cat waterfall in the late afternoon; Tram Ton Pass at sunset. September golden rice is the best month for terrace photography.

Phong Nha

Photo spots and best light: Inside Paradise Cave the boardwalk lighting is best in the morning, when the groups are thin; the Son River at sunset, from the bridge in town; the Bong Lai Valley in the late afternoon, when the light goes gold over the fields; and the Chay River from the Dark Cave zipline platform.

Nha Trang

Photo spots: The white Buddha viewpoint at Long Son Pagoda at golden hour; the cable car crossing to Hon Tre at sunset; Hon Chong Promontory at dusk; the fishing boats at Cau Da Port in the early morning; and the Tran Phu promenade at blue hour, when the city lights come on along the bay.

Da Lat

Photo spots and best light: Xuan Huong Lake at sunrise (mist on the water, 5:30–7:00); Langbiang summit in the late morning, when the clouds lift; Datanla Waterfall in the early morning before the crowds; the flower gardens in the dry season, in the soft light before 9:00.

Ho Chi Minh City

Photo spots: The Ben Thanh clock tower at dusk (long exposure from the roundabout); the Post Office interior in the morning light; the view from the Bitexco Skydeck at sunset; Cholon's Thien Hau Temple rooftop at golden hour; and the riverfront at Bach Dang Station, where the skyline meets the Saigon River.

Can Tho & Mekong Delta

Photo spots and best light: Cai Rang at sunrise (5:30–6:30), when the light is low and the market is busiest; the small canals in the mid-morning, when the light filters through the coconut palms; Ninh Kieu Wharf at sunset; and Tra Su forest in the morning, when birds are active.

Phu Quoc

Photo spots: Sao Beach in the early morning, before the day-trippers arrive; the Hon Thom cable car crossing at mid-morning, with the An Thoi archipelago below; the sun setting through the gap in the Kiss Bridge; the fishing boats at Ham Ninh village at dawn; and Long Beach at golden hour, when the whole west coast turns orange.
